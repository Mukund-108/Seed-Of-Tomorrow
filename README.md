Seeds of Tomorrow

A garden is never finished. It only keeps coming back.

Seeds of Tomorrow is a short visual novel with a garden-management game built into it, made in Ren'Py for the Hack Club Haven competition. The theme is Bounce Back.

You arrive at your late grandmother's house after failing the biggest thing you'd worked for. Her garden is overgrown and half dead. Over 12 days, storms, frost, pests, floods, drought and fire will destroy what you plant. Things will die, and your job is to plant again.

The idea

Bouncing back is the whole game, not just the story:

Every plant that dies can be cleared away for a seed and some compost, so loss gives something back.
Replanting in a spot where something died gives bonus resilience.
Resilience decides how your story ends, and how much your companion Moss grows.
How to play

Each day has three parts:

Morning: a short story scene with the people around the garden.
Garden: you have 3 actions to spend on the six plots.
Night: a disaster might strike, or a calm night might help the garden grow.
Garden actions
Action	Cost	What it does
Plant	1 action, 1 seed	Plants a seed in bare soil
Water	1 action	The plant grows overnight (sprout → growing → bloom takes two waterings)
Mulch	1 action, 1 compost	Protects a plant from one disaster
Collect seeds	1 action	Takes a seed from a plant in bloom
Clear	1 action	Removes a dead plant and gives +1 seed, +1 compost
Rest	Skip the day	Gives you an extra action tomorrow
Resilience

Resilience goes up when you:

Replant where something died (+2)
Prepare well enough that a disaster kills nothing (+1)
Make certain honest choices in the story
Disasters

Frost (Day 3), pests (Day 5), flood (Day 7), drought (Day 9) and wildfire (Day 11). Each one that you survive unlocks a page of your grandmother's journal.

Characters
You: the city kid who came to hide, and gets a chance to start again.
Moss: a shy, sarcastic plant spirit who wakes up when you care for the garden. He grows from a sprout to a sapling to a tree spirit as your resilience rises.
Tara: a curious village kid who wants to help.
Mr. Bose: Grandma Meera's old neighbor, who knows more than he says.
Dev: a friend from the city, heard only over the phone.
